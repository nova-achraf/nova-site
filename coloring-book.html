What this tool does

It builds the interior file of a paperback coloring book exactly the way Amazon KDP wants it for black-and-white printing: the page is the 8.5×11 in trim size plus 0.125 in of bleed on every side, so your artwork reaches the edge with no white slivers after trimming. Images are converted to grayscale at 300 DPI — the resolution KDP requires for line art.

Page count

  • Front matter: title page, copyright page, and one blank page.
  • Body: one page per drawing. With the blank option on, each drawing gets a blank page after it (55 drawings → 110 pages).
  • Back matter: a “thank you” page and a final blank page.

So 55 drawings with blank pages = 3 + 55 + 55 + 2 = 115 pages. KDP needs at least 24 pages for a paperback, and the count must be even for some print options — the counter above always tells you where you stand before exporting.

Print tips

  • Keep line art inside the safe area: 0.25 in inside the trim line.
  • Pure black lines on plain white give the cleanest print; avoid light gray shading.
  • Use the blank-page option for single-sided coloring so markers don’t bleed through.
